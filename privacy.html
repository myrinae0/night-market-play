Chimaek Night Market: privacy policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026.

Chimaek Night Market collects no data.

  • The app has no ads, no in-app purchases, no analytics, no crash reporting and no accounts.
  • It makes no network requests. The game runs entirely from the files inside the app.
  • Your progress and settings (levels, stars, coins, story choices, volumes, control options) are saved only on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere.
  • Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data, deletes that save.
  • No data is shared with anyone, and nothing is sold.
  • The app asks for no permissions beyond what every app has, and never uses your camera, microphone, location, contacts or files.

Chimaek Night Market is made and published by myrinae.
